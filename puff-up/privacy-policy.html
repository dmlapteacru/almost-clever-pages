Puff Up! Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Almost Clever Games handles information in the Android game Puff Up!, package name com.almostclever.puffup.

Overview

Puff Up! stores game progress and settings on your device. Core gameplay can be played offline and does not require an account. The game also uses Google Play Games Services for optional player sign-in, leaderboards, and Saved Games cloud synchronization, and Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertisements.

Almost Clever Games does not operate a separate player-account backend for the current version of Puff Up!, does not allow users to create an Almost Clever Games account in the app, and does not sell or rent personal information.

Google Play Games Services

If you choose to use Play Games Services, you sign in with an existing Google Play Games profile / Google Account created and managed by Google. Google processes your Play Games gamer identity, including a player identifier and, where available, a display name. Puff Up! uses this information for app functionality such as showing signed-in state, identifying your leaderboard entry, submitting scores, loading leaderboard results, and synchronizing game progress.

Use of Play Games Services is optional for Puff Up!'s core gameplay. In Google Play's Data Safety terminology, Play Games-related User IDs and certain Other app activity / gameplay actions may be collected and shared with Google for app functionality when you choose to use these services.

The Play Games Services SDK may also process service analytics and diagnostics for Google. Google handles this information under its own terms and privacy policies.

Leaderboards

Puff Up! can submit scores to Google Play Games Services and display weekly and all-time leaderboard information supplied by Google, including player display names, ranks, and scores. The visibility of Play Games profile and leaderboard information can depend on each player's Google Play Games privacy settings.

Game data and cloud saves

Puff Up! stores game data locally. When Play Games cloud synchronization is active, game data is also transmitted to and stored in Google Play Games Services Saved Games. This can include:

  • best scores, gameplay records, statistics, and progress counters;
  • sound, haptics, reduced-motion, language, and tutorial settings;
  • the currently equipped Puff;
  • earned in-game coins, owned Puffs, and wallet progress.

Saved Games is used to synchronize and recover progress across reinstalls or compatible devices signed in to the same Play Games profile.

Advertising and rewarded ads

Puff Up! uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide optional rewarded advertisements. When an eligible rewarded offer is available, you may choose whether to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward. Normal gameplay rewards do not require watching an advertisement.

After Google permits ad requests, Puff Up! may initialize the Mobile Ads SDK and preload a rewarded advertisement before you choose to watch it. As a result, the advertising SDK may process technical information even if you do not ultimately open a rewarded advertisement.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following categories of information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance:

  • Approximate location, inferred from your IP address; Puff Up! does not request Android location permission;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad or video interactions;
  • Diagnostics, such as information about app or SDK performance, launch time, hangs, and energy usage;
  • Device or other IDs, which can include the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers.

This information is transmitted to Google and may be shared by Google with advertising partners or service providers as applicable to ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Puff Up! does not send this advertising data to a separate server operated by Almost Clever Games.

Advertising ID and Android Privacy Sandbox

On supported Android devices, Google Mobile Ads may access the Android Advertising ID when it is available. The Advertising ID is controlled by Android and Google Play settings and may be reset, deleted, restricted, or unavailable depending on your device and privacy settings.

On supported Android versions, Google Mobile Ads may also use Android Privacy Sandbox advertising capabilities, including AdServices advertising identifier access, Attribution Reporting, and Topics, subject to Android, Google, regional, and user privacy controls.

Consent and privacy choices

Puff Up! uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tools. Privacy requirements and available choices are region-dependent. Where required, including under applicable European privacy rules and applicable U.S. state privacy rules, Google may present a consent or privacy message before Puff Up! requests advertisements.

Puff Up! checks whether Google permits ad requests before loading rewarded ads. Where Google requires privacy options to remain available, Puff Up! provides access to those choices from the game's settings. You can also manage relevant Google advertising and account privacy settings through your Google Account and Android device settings.

There is no single global consent control inside Puff Up! that disables every category of data processed by Google Mobile Ads for every user, device, and region. Depending on your region, device settings, account state, and privacy choices, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertising, or advertising may be unavailable.

Data Safety summary

Puff Up!'s Google Play Data Safety declaration reflects the current app and its included Google SDKs. It declares:

  • User IDs — collected and shared for app functionality when optional Play Games sign-in is used;
  • Other app activity / gameplay actions — collected and shared for app functionality when optional Play Games features are used;
  • Approximate location — collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance;
  • App interactions — collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance;
  • Diagnostics — collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance;
  • Device or other IDs — collected and shared for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Play Games-related User IDs and gameplay activity are optional because core gameplay works without Play Games sign-in. The Mobile Ads categories above are declared as required in the global Data Safety disclosure because their collection is part of the advertising SDK's operation when ad requests are allowed and there is no single opt-out available to every user in every region and on every device.

Purchases, financial features, health features, and analytics

The current version of Puff Up! contains no real-money in-app purchase or Google Play Billing integration and provides no financial features. Puffs acquired with earned in-game coins are part of game progression and are not real-money purchases.

Puff Up! does not provide health features and is not developed by or on behalf of a government.

Puff Up! does not currently use a developer-operated standalone analytics service. Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Games Services may process analytics, diagnostics, identifiers, and platform information as part of providing their respective services.

Network access

An internet connection is used for Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, Saved Games synchronization, consent and privacy messaging, and loading or displaying rewarded advertisements. Core gameplay remains available when these online services are unavailable.

Data security

User data transmitted by the Google services used by Puff Up! is encrypted in transit using secure protocols such as TLS/HTTPS. Puff Up! does not intentionally send passwords, payment-card details, or other payment credentials to Almost Clever Games.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing local app storage or uninstalling Puff Up! does not necessarily delete an existing Play Games Saved Game, so cloud progress may be restored after a later sign-in.

Puff Up! does not create or manage a developer-operated user account and therefore does not provide an Almost Clever Games account-deletion request flow. Google provides controls for deleting Play Games data, including data associated with an individual game, through Play Games or Google Account controls. Advertising information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention practices and the privacy controls available through Google, Android, and applicable consent interfaces.

Children's privacy

Puff Up!'s Google Play target audience is users aged 13 and over (13–15, 16–17, and 18+). The game is not intended for children under 13 and is not enrolled as a child-directed app. Puff Up! does not provide chat, user-generated social content, or direct account registration with Almost Clever Games. Google Play, Play Games Services, and Google Mobile Ads features are also governed by Google's account, privacy, advertising, and family controls.

Third-party services

Google processes information when you use Google Play, Google Play Games Services, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, Android advertising services, and related consent services. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's policies.
