Puff Up! Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Almost Clever Games handles information in the Android game Puff Up!, package name com.almostclever.puffup.

Information collection

Puff Up! does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information or user data.

The game does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, tracking, remote-configuration, social, or cloud-save services.

Local data stored on your device

Puff Up! may store the following game information locally on your device:

  • sound, haptics, and reduced-motion settings;
  • tutorial completion status;
  • best score and gameplay statistics;
  • local diagnostic information such as the most recent random seed, where enabled by the game.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to Almost Clever Games or to third parties.

Network access

Puff Up! is designed to work offline. The production version does not request the Android Internet permission and does not perform network operations.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of Puff Up! contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Puff Up! does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game has no chat, account creation, location tracking, personalized advertising, or user-generated content.

Deleting local data

You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the application's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

Third-party distribution platforms

Google Play may process information independently when you access, download, install, update, or review the game. That processing is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies and is not controlled by Puff Up!.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
