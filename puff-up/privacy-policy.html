Puff Up! Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Almost Clever Games handles information in the Android game Puff Up!, package name com.almostclever.puffup.

Overview

Puff Up! stores game progress and settings on your device. Core gameplay can be played offline. The game also uses Google Play Games Services for player sign-in, leaderboards, and Saved Games cloud synchronization, and Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertisements.

Almost Clever Games does not operate a separate player-account backend for the current version of Puff Up! and does not sell or rent personal information.

Google Play Games Services

When Play Games Services is available and you sign in, Google processes your Play Games gamer identity. Puff Up! may use your Play Games display name and player identifier to show your signed-in state, identify your leaderboard entry, submit scores, load leaderboard results, and synchronize game progress.

The Play Games Services SDK also processes service analytics and diagnostics for Google. Google handles this information under its own terms and privacy policies.

Leaderboards

Puff Up! can submit scores to Google Play Games Services and display weekly and all-time leaderboard information supplied by Google, including player display names, ranks, and scores. The visibility of Play Games profile and leaderboard information can depend on each player's Google Play Games privacy settings.

Game data and cloud saves

Puff Up! stores game data locally. When cloud synchronization is active, game data is also stored in Google Play Games Services Saved Games. This can include:

  • best scores, gameplay records, statistics, and progress counters;
  • sound, haptics, reduced-motion, language, and tutorial settings;
  • the currently equipped Puff;
  • earned in-game coins, owned Puffs, and wallet progress.

Saved Games is used to synchronize and recover progress across reinstalls or compatible devices signed in to the same Play Games profile.

Advertising and rewarded ads

Puff Up! uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide optional rewarded advertisements. When an eligible rewarded offer is available, you may choose whether to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward. Puff Up! does not require a real-money purchase to use rewarded ads.

After Google permits ad requests, Puff Up! may initialize the Mobile Ads SDK and preload a rewarded advertisement before you choose to watch it. As a result, the advertising SDK may process technical information even if you do not ultimately open a rewarded advertisement.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, information processed for advertising services can include:

  • your IP address and coarse or approximate location inferred from it;
  • app and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad or video interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device or account identifiers, which can include the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID when available.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Depending on your region, device settings, and privacy choices, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertising, or advertising may be unavailable.

Advertising ID

On supported Android devices, Google Mobile Ads may access the Android Advertising ID when it is available. The Advertising ID is controlled by Android and Google Play settings and may be reset, restricted, or unavailable depending on your device and privacy settings.

Consent and privacy choices

Puff Up! uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tools. Where required, Google may present a consent or privacy message before the game requests advertisements. Puff Up! checks whether Google permits ad requests before loading rewarded ads.

Where Google requires privacy options to be available, Puff Up! may provide access to those choices from the game's settings. You can also manage relevant Google advertising and account privacy settings through your Google account and device settings.

Purchases and analytics

The current version of Puff Up! contains no real-money in-app purchase or Google Play Billing integration. Puffs acquired with earned in-game coins are part of game progression and are not real-money purchases.

Puff Up! does not currently use a developer-operated standalone analytics service. Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Games Services may process analytics, diagnostics, identifiers, and platform information as part of providing their respective services.

Network access

An internet connection is used for Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, Saved Games synchronization, consent and privacy messaging, and loading or displaying rewarded advertisements. Core gameplay remains available when these online services are unavailable.

Data security

Online Google services used by Puff Up! transmit data using encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS. Puff Up! does not intentionally send passwords or payment-card details to Almost Clever Games.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing local app storage or uninstalling Puff Up! does not necessarily delete an existing Play Games Saved Game, so cloud progress may be restored after a later sign-in.

Google provides controls for deleting Play Games data, including data associated with an individual game, through Play Games or Google Account controls. Advertising information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention practices and the privacy controls available through Google, Android, and applicable consent interfaces.

Children's privacy

Puff Up!'s Google Play target audience is users aged 13 and over and the game is not intended for children under 13. Puff Up! does not provide chat, user-generated content, or direct account registration with Almost Clever Games. Google Play, Play Games Services, and Google Mobile Ads features are also governed by Google's account, privacy, advertising, and family controls.

Third-party services

Google processes information when you use Google Play, Google Play Games Services, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, and related consent services. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's policies.
