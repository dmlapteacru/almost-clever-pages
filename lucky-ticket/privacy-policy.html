Lucky Ticket Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Almost Clever Games handles information in the Android game Lucky Ticket, package name com.almostclever.luckyticket.

Overview

Lucky Ticket stores game data on your device and can use Google Play Games Services for player sign-in and Saved Games cloud synchronization. The game also uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases.

Almost Clever Games does not sell or rent personal information. The current version contains no advertising SDK and no developer-operated third-party analytics service.

Google Play Games Services

When Play Games Services is available and you sign in, Google may process your Play Games gamer identity and service information. Lucky Ticket may access your Play Games player name to show your signed-in state and uses your authenticated Play Games profile to read and write the game's Saved Games data.

The Play Games Services SDK may also collect diagnostics and analytics for Google's service stability and product improvement. This processing is performed by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Game data stored locally and in Saved Games

Lucky Ticket stores game state locally on your device. When cloud synchronization is active, a version of this game state may also be stored in Google Play Games Services Saved Games. Depending on your progress and features used, the saved state can include:

  • gameplay progress, records, statistics and session state;
  • settings, language and onboarding/completion state;
  • campaign, Daily, achievement and unlock progress;
  • owned and equipped cosmetic items;
  • the in-game currency balance and wallet state;
  • purchase-related identifiers and delivery records needed to prevent the same Google Play purchase from being credited more than once.

The cloud save is used to synchronize and recover Lucky Ticket game state across reinstalls or compatible devices signed in to the same Play Games profile.

In-app purchases

Lucky Ticket offers optional consumable in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment transaction. Almost Clever Games does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

For a purchase, Lucky Ticket can receive transaction information from Google Play such as the purchased product, purchase status and a unique purchase token. The game stores purchase-token/delivery information locally and, when cloud synchronization is active, in the Saved Games state. This information is used to grant purchased in-game currency, prevent duplicate delivery and recover unfinished purchases safely.

Network access

Core gameplay can be used with locally stored state, but network access is required for Google Play Games sign-in/cloud synchronization and Google Play Billing operations. Availability of those services depends on Google Play and the user's device/account configuration.

Advertising and developer analytics

The current version of Lucky Ticket does not display third-party advertising and does not use a developer-operated advertising or analytics SDK. Google Play and Play Games Services may process service diagnostics, analytics, account, purchase and platform information independently as part of providing their services.

Data security

Cloud synchronization and purchase operations use Google Play platform services. Google states that Play Games Services encrypts collected end-user data in transit using HTTPS. Lucky Ticket does not intentionally transmit passwords or payment-card details to Almost Clever Games.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until it is cleared, removed by the game where applicable, or the application is uninstalled. Clearing local application storage or uninstalling Lucky Ticket does not necessarily delete an existing Play Games Saved Game, and cloud state may be restored after a later sign-in.

Purchase-token/delivery identifiers may be retained with the game state for as long as needed to prevent duplicate purchase credits and preserve wallet integrity.

Google provides controls for deleting Play Games data, including data associated with an individual game, through the user's Play Games profile or Google account controls. For help with Lucky Ticket data or a privacy request, contact Almost Clever Games using the address below.

Children's privacy

Lucky Ticket does not provide chat, user-generated content, personalized advertising, or direct account registration with Almost Clever Games. Google Play and Play Games Services account features are governed by Google's account, privacy and family controls.

Third-party services

Google processes information when you use Google Play, Play Games Services or Google Play Billing. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's policies. You can review the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Lucky Ticket's features, service providers or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
